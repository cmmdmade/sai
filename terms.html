7. From My Wildest Dreams
This section applies to the From My Wildest Dreams project. It is the offer described on the project page and at checkout, and it controls where it differs from the general terms elsewhere in this document.

7.1 What you are buying
From My Wildest Dreams is a fixed-fee project for one recurring task.

You bring one task you repeat in your business. We write a reusable AI prompt for that task and test it before we meet. In the session we paste it into your own Claude Project, Google Gem, or ChatGPT plugin, run it on your own material, and adjust what does not fit. The prompt is what you are buying. The Project, Gem, or plugin is where you keep it, inside an account you already hold and pay for. The session is one private 90-minute video meeting, recorded, offered Tuesday to Friday in Eastern time and no earlier than three business days after your answers are received. The last ten minutes go on a short written brief for a related task you want to tackle yourself later.

One prompt's worth of work means one task, one kind of input, one kind of output, for one audience; the information the task needs can be supplied to the AI or already sits in your account; and nothing in the task depends on a system we cannot reach. Two outputs, two audiences, or two separate jobs is two projects.

You keep:

Your master prompt as an editable document.
The Prompt Workbench: one completed example from your project and a blank copy for the next one.
Your project brief and the session deck prepared for your session.
One written Second Look within 30 days, as described in section 7.6.
The fee covers one task, one prompt, and one session. A second task is a second project.

The fee does not include an AI subscription, API credits, third-party software, or work outside the one task you brought.

7.2 Payment, and the questions that open after it
The project fee is $450. A founding-client code, while it remains available, takes $100 off, for a fee of $350.

Payment is taken before the full questionnaire. The short first form collects your contact details and your consent, and costs nothing. The full questions, which take about ten minutes, open after the payment has gone through.

The payment is collected through Stripe on a single-use payment link tied to your project. That keeps an unpaid request from being used to obtain the questionnaire, and it lets us close a link that belongs to one closed project without affecting anyone else's.

When your answers arrive, we check your brief against what one prompt can be, as defined in section 7.1. That check decides whether the task fits, it happens after payment and after your answers, and you hear the outcome within one business day. Your booking link is sent once the check is confirmed, not before, so you do not book a session for a task we are about to move to credit.

If you have paid and the questions do not arrive, email renee.leach@neutral3rd.space and we will resend them.
