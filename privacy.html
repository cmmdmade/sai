1. Introduction
Neutral3rd ("Neutral3rd," "we," "our," or "us") is an independent emerging technologies development studio providing artificial intelligence (AI) enablement, software development, automation, digital communications, content systems, accessibility, and related professional services. Neutral3rd is a subsidiary brand of Rowdy Independents LLC and operates from the United States.

We recognize that privacy, transparency, and responsible data handling are essential to building trustworthy digital experiences.

This Privacy Policy explains how personal information is collected, used, disclosed, retained, and protected when individuals visit this website, contact us, purchase products or services, participate in consultations, or interact with digital solutions that we operate.

It covers both fixed-scope service offers described in our Terms of Service: From My Wildest Dreams and Fast, Good & Cheap.

This policy applies to this web space (https://neutral3rd.space/) and other Neutral3rd-operated services that expressly link to it. A separately operated product, client implementation, or third-party platform may have its own privacy notice.

By using this website, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy. Where applicable law requires consent, we will seek it separately.

4.5 Automated Intake Review for Fast, Good & Cheap
The Fast, Good & Cheap request form uses an automated review to compare the notes you send against the questions for that service. The review records which questions your notes already answer, so the follow-up email asks only what is still missing.

The review classifies intake only. It does not price the job, decide the scope, judge whether you are a good fit, or write the deliverable. A person reviews the brief before production, and the finished work is checked by a person before delivery.

6. Payments & Transactions
Neutral3rd uses third-party payment processors, including Stripe, to facilitate purchases, subscriptions, invoices, and refunds.

Payment processors collect payment card information, billing addresses, transaction identifiers, and fraud-prevention information under their own terms and privacy policies. Card numbers are entered on the processor's page and are not stored by Neutral3rd.

We receive transaction confirmations, payment status, the name, business name, email address, billing address and telephone number you supply at checkout, and limited payment-method information such as the card brand and last four digits, as needed for customer service, accounting, and dispute records.

We do not collect or store complete payment card numbers through our website, forms, or email.

Where an offer states that a fee is not refundable in cash and instead becomes credit, that term is set out in our Terms of Service, sections 7.3 and 8.6, and is shown before you pay.

Where the check described in section 7 of our Terms finds that a task is not one prompt's worth of work, or a Fast, Good & Cheap project closes to credit under section 8.6 of our Terms, the outcome is recorded as credit against the name and email address that paid. That record is kept with the payment record so the credit can be honoured later.

7. From My Wildest Dreams: What We Hold
This section describes the data handled by the From My Wildest Dreams project. It adds detail to the sections above; it does not replace them.

7.1 What you send before paying
The short first form asks for your name, business name, email address, website, and a description of the task you want a prompt for. It also records your agreement to the project terms, with the date and time.

Checkout adds the billing details your card issuer requires, as described in section 6.

7.2 What you send after paying
Once payment has gone through, the full questionnaire asks about your business, the task, the rules and exceptions the prompt must follow, and examples of your work. You may also upload files or paste examples.

Those answers are used to write your brief, build and test your prompt, prepare your session, and produce the documents you keep.

When your answers arrive we review them against what one prompt can be and record the outcome: that the task fits, or that it becomes credit. You hear that outcome within one business day. The booking link is sent after the outcome, so a session is only booked for a task that has passed the check.

If you book a session, the booking records your chosen time in our calendar, together with your name and email address.

7.3 What we create
For each project we create a folder in our Google Drive containing your brief, the documents prepared for your session, your prompt and workbench files, and a record of the payment.

The payment record holds the amount, the date, the processor's transaction reference, the terms you accepted, and confirmation that the terms were shown to you. It exists so that a question about the payment can be answered from the record.

The prompt we write is delivered into your own Claude, Google, or OpenAI account, together with the documents you keep. That account belongs to you and is governed by that provider's terms. We do not hold your login for it, and we cannot see the account after the session unless you share it with us.

7.4 How long we keep it
- Project folder and deliverables: kept while the project is active and for three years after delivery, for support, the Second Look, and any dispute.
- Payment records: kept for seven years, which covers tax and accounting requirements.
- Session booking: held in the calendar for the period above.
- Emails: held in our mail account for the period above unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
- Second Look: the 30-day window closes on schedule whether or not it is used.
- Check outcome and credit records: kept with the project and payment records, for the periods above, so a credit is not lost and a scope question can be answered later. Credit expires 12 months after the original payment, as Terms section 7.3 states.
- Session recordings: kept in the project folder for the project period above, unless you ask us to delete one sooner.

7.5 What happens to an unpaid project
A request that has not been paid is held for up to three days, with up to three reminders. If it is still unpaid after that, the project is closed, the working folder created for it is removed, and any single-use payment link for it is cancelled.

A removed folder goes to the Drive trash and can be restored for up to 30 days before it is purged. The record that you made a request, and that it was closed, stays in our project list.

You can ask us to delete that record at any time, subject to section 12.

7.6 Session recordings
Project sessions are recorded and the recording is shared with you. It is kept in your project folder for the period in section 7.4.

A recording captures what is shown on screen during the session, the voices in it, your camera picture if your camera is on, and any material either of us shows. It is used for the same purposes as the rest of the project record: to deliver the session, to answer a question afterwards, and to resolve a dispute. We do not publish a recording without your written consent.

If you do not want a session recorded, tell us before the session is booked and we will tell you what is possible before you pay. You may also ask us to delete a recording earlier than section 7.4 provides, subject to section 12.

8. Fast, Good & Cheap: What We Hold
This section describes the data handled by a Fast, Good & Cheap project. It adds detail to the sections above; it does not replace them.

8.1 What you send before paying
The request form asks for your name, email address, business or project name, website or social link, the service and tradeoff you chose, and your project details. It records your agreement to the terms, with the date and time.

The form does not take payment. The Stripe payment link is sent by email.

8.2 What you send after paying
After payment we ask only the questions your request did not already answer. You may send written answers, links and files. Those answers and materials are used to produce, check and deliver the document you bought, and to answer a question about it afterwards.

8.3 What we create
For each project we create a folder in our Google Drive with four parts: the request, the supplied material, working drafts, and the final delivery. The folder holds your brief, the deliverable, and the payment record.

A row in the pipeline sheet records the service, tradeoff, price, status and dates. It does not hold the contents of your files.

If a credit is issued, the credit record is kept with the payment record, as described in section 8.5.

8.4 How long we keep it
- Project folder and deliverables: kept while the project is active and for three years after delivery, for support and any dispute.
- Payment records: kept for seven years, which covers tax and accounting requirements.
- Emails: held in our mail account for the period above unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
- Automated intake review result: kept with the project record for the period above, so a scope question can be answered later.
- Credit records: kept with the payment record. Credit expires 12 months after the original payment, as Terms section 8.6 states.

8.5 If a project closes without delivery
If a project closes without delivery, the materials are held under section 8.4 and deleted earlier on request, subject to records we must keep.

The record that the request was made and closed stays in the project list. If the fee becomes credit, the credit record stays with the payment record so the credit can be honoured.
