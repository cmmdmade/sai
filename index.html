FAQ

What is an AI Business Guide?

An AI Business Guide is a conversational assistant configured around your approved business information. It helps visitors find answers, understand your services, and identify relevant next steps.

How is it different from a conventional chatbot?

Rather than relying only on fixed question-and-answer scripts, the guide is configured to respond conversationally using relevant business information. Its behavior and available actions depend on the setup and supported integrations.

What information do you need from me?

We begin with your website, business description, products or services, frequently asked questions, contact details, and desired customer actions. We'll identify any additional information needed during discovery.

Can it work with my existing website?

Website integration is one of the intended deployment options. We'll review your website platform, available integration methods, and requirements before recommending the right setup.

How do I get started?

Request early access or start a conversation with Neutral3rd. We'll discuss your business, what you want the guide to accomplish, and whether the current development offering is a suitable fit.

How much does an AI Business Guide cost?

Pricing depends on your business requirements, information sources, integrations, and support needs. Neutral3rd provides a tailored scope and pricing information after an initial discussion.

How do you help keep its answers accurate?

We use approved business information, test representative questions, review responses, and refine the configuration. Because AI can make mistakes, periodic review and updated source information remain important.

What happens after my AI Business Guide launches?

Ongoing services may include updating business information, reviewing conversations, testing changes, and refining the experience. The specific support arrangements are established as part of your engagement.
