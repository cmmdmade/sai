FAQ

What is Converza by Neutral3rd?

Converza is a business-specific AI-based conversational experience that helps customers and potential customers explore offerings, ask questions, compare documented options, and find relevant next steps using information approved by your business.

Where can customers use Converza?

Depending on the selected package and supported integrations, Converza can be configured for your website, AI assistants such as ChatGPT and Claude, and supported messaging channels. Channel availability and requirements are confirmed before deployment.

How is Converza different from a basic chatbot?

Converza is configured around your business information and customer journeys rather than only a fixed script. Supported capabilities can include product or service discovery, comparisons, verified links, and connected data. Available features depend on your configuration.

Can Converza connect to my products or business systems?

Supported options may include product feeds, Google Sheets, Shopify, CSV files, or a custom API. Your business' existing systems, data quality, and connector compatibility are assessed before recommending an integration. Live inventory or automatic synchronization is not assumed.

What do you need from my business to get started?

Configuration begins with your business website, product or services listings, frequently asked questions, policies, approved contact details, and the actions you want customers to take. Any catalog access, branding assets, or additional data requirements are confirmed during onboarding.

How much does Converza cost?

Converza is offered as a managed setup with ongoing support. Pricing depends on the selected package, channels, information sources, integrations, and maintenance scope. The setup fee, recurring charges, and any optional work are confirmed before you commit.

How do you help keep Converza's answers reliable?

This application works from approved business information, test representative customer questions, review results, and makes documented updates. AI responses can still be incorrect, so source accuracy, periodic checks, and clear paths to human support remain important.

What happens after launch, and who manages the experience?

Neutral3rd has the capability to manage configuration updates, scheduled checks, testing, and agreed maintenance. Publishing identity, platform access, client responsibilities, and support terms are established during setup. Additional channels or features are scoped separately.
