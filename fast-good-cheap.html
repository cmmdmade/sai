How it works

Pick two. Understand the tradeoff.

The classic rule is simple: you can prioritize two, but the third gives way. Every delivered item must still be accurate, usable and reviewed. “Not good” means less polish and depth, never careless or defective work.

FAST + GOODNOT CHEAP

Benefit: priority scheduling, full scope and deeper refinement.
Tradeoff: premium price for reserved production capacity.

GOOD + CHEAPNOT FAST

Benefit: full scope and thoughtful refinement at a lower starting price.
Tradeoff: flexible scheduling; 3–14 business days estimated.

CHEAP + FASTNOT FULL-SERVICE GOOD

Benefit: a quick, affordable starting point.
Tradeoff: limited depth, templated structure, minimal customization and no extensive revision cycle. Still fact-checked and usable.
