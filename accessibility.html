1. Our Commitment to Accessibility

At Neutral3rd, we believe technology should make work and everyday experiences more accessible, understandable, and effective.

Accessibility is an important part of our approach to developing digital communications, software, artificial intelligence solutions, and business technology systems.

We are committed to improving the accessibility and usability of our website for people with diverse abilities, needs, technologies, and ways of interacting with digital content.

Our objective is to provide an inclusive experience for visitors, prospective clients, customers, and individuals who use assistive technologies.

We recognize that accessibility is an ongoing process involving design, development, testing, feedback, and continuous improvement.

2. Accessibility Standards

Neutral3rd uses the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) as a reference for accessible digital design and development.

Our accessibility target for this website is WCAG 2.2 Level AA.

These internationally recognized guidelines address accessibility needs involving vision, hearing, mobility, cognition, and other disabilities.

The guidelines are organized around four foundational principles:

- Perceivable: Information and interface components should be presented in ways users can perceive.
- Operable: Navigation and interface components should be usable through different interaction methods.
- Understandable: Information and interface behavior should be clear and predictable.
- Robust: Content should be compatible with appropriate browsers, devices, and assistive technologies.

These principles inform our accessibility improvement efforts.

3. Current Accessibility Status

Neutral3rd is working toward its stated accessibility target.

A complete, documented evaluation establishing WCAG 2.2 Level AA conformance across the entire website has not been confirmed for purposes of this statement.

Accordingly, this statement should not be interpreted as a certification or declaration that every page, component, or third-party integration fully conforms to WCAG 2.2 Level AA.

We intend to review accessibility concerns, evaluate barriers, and prioritize improvements based on their effect on users.

4. Accessibility Considerations

Our accessibility approach considers the following areas.

4.1 Keyboard Accessibility
Interactive controls, links, navigation, and forms should be usable without requiring a mouse.

We seek to identify and address keyboard-access barriers, including inaccessible controls, missing focus indicators, and focus-management problems.

4.2 Screen Reader Compatibility
We aim to use meaningful content structure, headings, accessible names, labels, and semantic markup to support assistive technologies.

Screen reader behavior may vary depending on the device, browser, software, and individual configuration.

4.3 Readability & Visual Presentation
We consider text contrast, typography, spacing, responsive layouts, and information hierarchy when developing website content.

Our goal is to make information easier to read and navigate across desktop and mobile devices.

4.4 Images & Non-Text Content
Meaningful images should have appropriate text alternatives where necessary to communicate their purpose.

Decorative images should be implemented so that they do not create unnecessary barriers for assistive technology users.

4.5 Forms & Interactive Elements
Forms should provide understandable labels, instructions, error identification, and feedback.

We aim to improve the usability of contact forms, inquiry processes, buttons, and other interactive elements.

4.6 Motion & Multimedia
Where animations, video, audio, or other multimedia features are used, we consider accessibility requirements such as captions, alternatives, motion sensitivity, and user control.

4.7 Responsive & Mobile Accessibility
Our website is intended to support a range of screen sizes and input methods.

We consider text resizing, content reflow, touch-target usability, and navigation behavior as part of accessibility improvement work.

5. Artificial Intelligence & Accessibility

Neutral3rd develops and implements AI-enabled tools and conversational interfaces.

We recognize that AI technologies can both improve access to information and introduce new accessibility challenges.

Relevant considerations include:

- Accessible chat and messaging interfaces.
- Keyboard navigation through conversational controls.
- Meaningful labels for input fields and action buttons.
- Clear communication of system responses and errors.
- Readable and understandable generated content.
- Appropriate focus management and status announcements.
- Alternatives when automated interactions are inaccessible.
- Human review of important or consequential AI-generated information.

The accessibility of third-party AI platforms and embedded interfaces may depend on their own design, implementation, and ongoing updates.

Neutral3rd does not represent that every third-party AI component is fully accessible.



6. Known & Potential Limitations

Although we aim to improve accessibility, some visitors may encounter barriers.

Areas that may require additional evaluation include:

- Third-party embedded widgets or conversational interfaces.
- Navigation and menu behavior on certain devices.
- Keyboard focus behavior in interactive components.
- Images or visual content that require improved alternatives.
- Video or audio materials requiring accessible equivalents.
- Forms and automated status messages.
- Components affected by changes to browsers, themes, or external services.

These are areas identified for evaluation, not a declaration that each listed issue has been confirmed on the live website.

We encourage users to report specific barriers so they can be investigated.

7. Third-Party Content & Services

Our website may include content or functionality supplied by external providers.

Examples may include scheduling tools, payment services, AI interfaces, videos, social platforms, and other embedded technologies.

We may not control every aspect of third-party accessibility.

Where a third-party component creates an accessibility barrier, we will consider available alternatives, configuration changes, workarounds, or communication with the provider.

8. Requesting Accessibility Assistance

If you have difficulty accessing any information, feature, document, or service on the Neutral3rd website, please contact us.

Email: support@neutral3rd.space

To help us investigate, please provide any information you are comfortable sharing, such as:

- The page or feature involved.
- A description of the difficulty.
- The task you were attempting to complete.
- Your browser, device, or assistive technology, if relevant.
- An alternative format or method of assistance that would be useful.

You are not required to disclose a disability diagnosis or sensitive medical information to report an accessibility problem.

We will review reported issues and seek to provide reasonable assistance or an accessible alternative when feasible.

9. Alternative Access to Information

If website content or an interactive feature is inaccessible, you may request assistance by email.

Depending on the content and circumstances, we may be able to provide information in an alternative electronic format, explain the relevant material, or offer another practical method of communication.

Availability of a particular alternative may depend on the content, technology, and resources involved.

10. Accessibility Evaluation & Improvement

Our accessibility improvement process may include:

- Reviewing website structure and semantic markup.
- Checking keyboard navigation and visible focus.
- Evaluating text and non-text contrast.
- Reviewing images and text alternatives.
- Testing forms and interactive elements.
- Considering screen reader compatibility.
- Evaluating responsive behavior and content reflow.
- Reviewing multimedia accessibility.
- Assessing relevant third-party integrations.
- Prioritizing reported barriers and remediation opportunities.

Automated accessibility testing can assist in identifying certain issues but cannot establish complete accessibility on its own.

Manual evaluation and testing with assistive technologies are also important.

11. Accessibility in Client Projects

Accessibility is a service consideration within Neutral3rd's development and consulting work.

For client projects, the applicable agreement should define the accessibility requirements, technical standards, testing methods, responsibilities, and acceptance criteria.

Where requested, accessibility-related services may include design review, technical assessment, remediation guidance, usability considerations, and accessibility documentation.

Any formal conformance claim or certification must be supported by appropriate evaluation evidence.

12. Continuous Improvement

Accessibility requirements, assistive technologies, and digital platforms continue to evolve.

We intend to review this statement and our accessibility practices as the website changes.

Feedback from people who experience accessibility barriers is an important source of information for identifying improvements.

We welcome constructive reports and suggestions.
